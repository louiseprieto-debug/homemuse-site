The process

From vision to plan.

Six steps, one person from start to finish. That's me.

  1. Pre-session questionnaire

    So I arrive knowing your space, your life and your vision.

  2. In-depth space audit

    In person or by video, every room read with fresh eyes: flow, light, energy, potential.

  3. Creative direction

    We find the aesthetic that truly belongs to you.

  4. Design plans and mood board

    Layouts, materials, palette and furniture direction, ready to share with artisans and suppliers.

  5. Report and roadmap

    Budget breakdown, prioritised action list and sourcing advice.

  6. Follow-up a week later

    We review progress so nothing gets lost between inspiration and action.

Questions

How quickly can you start?

Tell me your listing or moving date on our call, and I'll tell you honestly whether we can make it.

Do you work with my estate agent?

Happily. I can coordinate with your agent so the staging and the listing photos tell the same story.

Do I need to buy new furniture?

Often, no. Many transformations come from decluttering and rearranging what you already own. Where a purchase makes a real difference, I'll say so and suggest where to save.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. Audits and design plans work beautifully by video, wherever you are in the world.
