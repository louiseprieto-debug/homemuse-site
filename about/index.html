About

Designer, coordinator, creative partner.

My path into interiors began somewhere between a Parisian atelier and a hardware store in the South of France. That contrast tells you everything about how I work.

Your home is your third skin.

Beyond your body and your clothes, it's the environment that holds you. It shapes your mornings, restores you after difficult days, and quietly tells the world, and you, who you're becoming.

I bring the rigour of fashion to the warmth of home. My approach draws on holistic design, Feng Shui and personal development, and I handle the complex, the tedious and the overwhelming so the process feels like the joyful transformation it should be.

You'll find me on my yoga mat at dawn, lost in a flower shop, or obsessing over tile samples at a trade fair. Always working towards the same thing: a home that fits you, like the perfect outfit.

Warmly, Lou · Founder, Home Muse

My approach

Listen. Reveal. Transform.

  1. Listen

    Every project begins with a real conversation. Not about trends, about you and how you want to feel at home.

  2. Reveal

    Most clients already know what they want. They just haven't had the space to say it. That clarity is what I help you find.

  3. Transform

    Then we build it, with intention, precision, and a holistic thread through every material and every detail.
